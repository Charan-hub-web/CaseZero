Three days ago, award-winning journalist Ezhilan vanished from his flat on a rainy Tuesday night. The door was unlocked. His laptop was still running, a cup of tea had gone cold beside it, and his phone was gone. On screen was a half-typed message that was never sent.

The police shrugged it off as a man who wanted to disappear. Then an envelope arrived at the station, with no stamp and no name:

"HE WAS ABOUT TO PRINT THE TRUTH. THE PERSON YOU WANT SMILED AT HIM THAT NIGHT."

You are the lead detective. Several people have stories. Some of them are true.

CASE SOLVED

HARSHINI IS GUILTY

MOTIVE: As treasurer of the Asha Trust, Harshini had been quietly forging a signature on its withdrawals. Ezhilan, her closest friend, found out. He gave her one night to explain before he published. She used it to silence him.

  • Clue 01 – The notes file: Ezhilan wrote that one signature was forged and that he owed "her" a chance to explain at nine that night. Three of the suspects are women, so this alone proved nothing.
  • Clue 02 – The timeline: A visitor arrived at 8:30, long before Deepti's car left at 10:30. Ezhilan's phone then left the building at 10:50, after Deepti was gone.
  • Clue 03 – The coded note: "Ask who keeps the books." Deepti keeps the company books, but the note lay beside the Trust's bank statement.
  • Clue 04 – The matches: Deepti's lie covered a secret sale of the company. Charan and Deepthika were cleared by logs. Only Harshini's Trust card paid for a cab to Apartment 404, and the party host says she arrived at 11 PM.
